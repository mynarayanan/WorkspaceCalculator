Model note: Anaplan does not publish its internal workspace-sizing formula. This tool approximates cell volume as (list dimension sizes) × (time periods) × (versions) × (1 + summary overhead), then applies a per-data-type byte weight for an indicative MB figure. Use this for directional "before vs after" planning — validate final numbers against Anaplan's own workspace usage screen before making capacity or licensing decisions.
